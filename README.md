# E-Commerce Performance Dashboard (Power BI + SQL)

## 📊 Project Overview
This project presents an interactive E-Commerce Performance Dashboard built in Power BI to analyze revenue trends, category performance, and customer behavior.

The project follows an end-to-end analytics workflow, starting from data cleaning and transformation in SQL to dashboard development and business insight generation in Power BI.

The objective was not only to visualize data, but also to uncover meaningful business insights and provide actionable recommendations.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives
- Track overall business performance using key KPIs
- Analyze revenue, orders, and customer growth trends
- Identify top-performing product categories
- Understand customer acquisition and retention behavior
- Generate business recommendations from data insights

---

## 🧹 Data Cleaning & Preparation (SQL)
Before visualization, the dataset was cleaned and transformed using SQL.

### Key Cleaning Steps:
- Handled missing and inconsistent values
- Filtered invalid and duplicate records
- Structured datasets for analysis
- Prepared clean tables for Power BI integration
- Standardized date and category fields

This process ensured the dashboard was built on reliable and analysis-ready data.

---

## 🛠 Tools & Technologies Used
- SQL (Data Cleaning & Transformation)
- Power BI (Dashboard Development & Visualization)
- DAX (KPIs & Calculated Measures)

---

# 📌 Dashboard Pages

## 1️⃣ Performance Overview
This page provides a high-level summary of business performance.

### Key Metrics:
- Average Order Value (AOV)
- Total Revenue
- Total Orders
- Total Customers

### Insights:
- Revenue and orders showed consistent growth over time
- Peak performance was observed in 2018
- Customer growth increased steadily alongside order volume

---

## 2️⃣ Category Performance Analysis
This page analyzes performance across product categories.

### Visuals Included:
- Revenue by Category
- Orders by Category
- Customers by Category

### Insights:
- A few categories contribute significantly more revenue than others
- Certain product categories drive customer acquisition and order volume
- Category performance varies, revealing opportunities for optimization

---

## 3️⃣ Customer Insights & Retention Analysis
This page focuses on customer behavior and retention trends.

### Visuals Included:
- AOV by Category
- Customer Distribution (New vs Repeat)
- Customer Growth Trend

### Key Findings:
- Approximately 97% of customers are new
- Only about 2% are repeat customers
- Growth is heavily driven by acquisition rather than retention

### Business Implication:
Low retention suggests weak customer loyalty, which may increase dependence on continuous customer acquisition.

### Recommendations:
- Implement customer loyalty programs
- Use personalized marketing campaigns
- Encourage repeat purchases through targeted promotions
- Focus on high AOV categories to maximize customer value

---

## 💡 Key Business Insights
- Revenue, orders, and customer growth all increased steadily over time
- Business performance is driven by a few strong product categories
- Customer retention is significantly lower than acquisition
- Improving retention could increase long-term profitability and customer lifetime value
